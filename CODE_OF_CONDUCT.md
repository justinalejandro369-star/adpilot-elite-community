# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project.

Participants are expected to communicate constructively, welcome different levels of experience, respect privacy, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, or publishing another person's confidential information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the community. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel; do not expose sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect reporters from retaliation where possible.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and project-affiliated spaces.
